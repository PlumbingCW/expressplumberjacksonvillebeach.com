What we do

Jacksonville Beach Express Plumbing answers calls at 4106 3rd St S and routes each one to a licensed plumber who handles that kind of work. Emergencies such as burst pipes, sewer backups and failed water heaters go to the front of the line, and planned jobs are booked into same-day appointments when available.

The work covers drains, water heaters, leak finding, sewer lines, toilets, faucets, disposals, repiping and new installations. You can see every service on the services page, and the Neptune Beach page covers the town next door.

How pricing and approval work

A plumber looks at the problem first and gives you a clear price before work starts. If opening a wall or lifting a fixture changes the job, you hear about it and approve the change before anything more is done.

We do not publish price lists, because a clogged line and a collapsed line can look identical from the outside. The honest answer comes after someone has seen the pipe.

Checking any plumber in Florida

Florida contractors are licensed under Chapter 489 of the Florida Statutes, and each construction business must be qualified by a properly licensed individual contractor. The state's MyFloridaLicense.com site has a Verify a License tool that searches by license number, individual name or business name.

Use it on us and on anyone else you invite into your house. For permit questions on a specific job, the City's Building Inspection Division at 11 North Third Street (904-247-6100) is the right office.

What we will not do

Our honest limits

  • We do not diagnose gas leaks over the phone. If you smell gas, leave and call your gas utility or 911 first.
  • We do not promise an arrival time, because the road, the weather and the job itself all change it.
  • We do not make health claims about tap water. Ask the City Water Division for its annual report, and talk to your doctor about health questions.
  • We do not quote a price sight unseen for work that depends on what is behind a wall.

How a call turns into a visit

A dispatcher asks where the water is, whether the main is closed and what you can see or hear. Those answers decide whether the job is an emergency, a same-day appointment or a planned visit, and they decide which plumber goes.

The plumber who arrives checks the cause before quoting. A slow tub drain might be a hair clog in the trap, or it might be a main line problem that shows up in every fixture, and the price follows the answer.

Why local knowledge matters on the Beaches

Jacksonville Beach sits on San Pablo Island, with much of its land at or near sea level and a hurricane season that runs June 1 to November 30. After Hurricane Matthew in October 2016 brought major flooding and a mandatory evacuation, many homeowners learned how much a backed-up line or a failed water heater costs in a flooded week.

The City runs its own water and sewer utility from six wells in the Floridan Aquifer, so we know which problems belong to the Water Division and which belong to a plumber. That one distinction saves most callers an hour on a bad night. If the leak is on your side of the meter, read about water line repair before you call.
