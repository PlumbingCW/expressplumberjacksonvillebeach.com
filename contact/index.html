What to have ready when you call

  • The street address and nearest cross street or landmark.
  • What you see, hear or smell, and when it started.
  • Whether the main water shutoff is closed and whether it stopped the water.
  • A photo of any label on the water heater, if the call is about hot water.

With those details dispatch can match the right plumber to the job. For burst lines, start with burst pipe repair, and for a blocked line, drain cleaning explains what happens next.

If the problem is in the street

Water coming from the city main or the meter is the City's responsibility. The Jacksonville Beach Water Division lists 904-247-6241 for its main office (Monday to Friday, 8:00 to 4:30) and 904-247-6278 as its emergency number. For Neptune Beach addresses, the City runs its own water and its City Hall number is 904-270-2400.

If you smell natural gas or propane, leave the building and call your gas utility or 911 first.
