Our home market runs from the oceanfront near the pier, Casa Marina Hotel and Seawalk Pavilion, west along Beach Boulevard (US 90) and down State Road A1A, which is 3rd Street through town. It also takes in the south side along Butler Boulevard (SR 202) and the shopping near South Beach Regional.

What changes from one town to the next

The plumbing is similar across the Beaches, but the offices you deal with are not. Jacksonville Beach and Neptune Beach each run their own water and sewer systems, and each has its own permit office, so a water heater swap or a re-pipe follows a different paper trail depending on the address.

In Jacksonville Beach, plumbing permits go through the Building Inspection Division at 11 North Third Street (904-247-6100), and the City states that work may not begin until a permit is approved and posted on site. In Neptune Beach, permits are listed by Planning and Community Development at 116 First Street (904-270-2400). Ask the right office about your job before the plumber starts.

Emergency dispatch across the Beaches

The emergency line is open 24/7 for every town above. A burst supply line in an Atlantic Beach kitchen, a backed-up main in a Neptune Beach townhouse and a failed water heater near the Jacksonville Beach pier all reach the same dispatcher, who sends the nearest plumber that handles that kind of work.

Ponte Vedra Beach addresses in ZIP codes 32082 and 32004 are served too. Because they sit in St. Johns County, we confirm the address and the water provider on the call, so the plumber arrives knowing which utility to contact if the problem is at the meter.
