Water is backing up right now: what to do first

Find out how many fixtures are affected. If only one drain is slow or full, the clog is in that fixture's own branch line. If the shower gurgles when the toilet flushes, or the tub fills when the washer drains, the blockage is farther downstream in the main line, and every minute of water use adds to the problem.

While you wait for the plumber

  • Stop using every sink, tub, toilet and the washing machine until the line is clear.
  • Do not pour chemical drain opener into a standing blockage. It sits in the pipe and burns whoever opens it next.
  • Move rugs and electronics away from the lowest drain, usually a tub, shower or floor drain.
  • Take a photo of any water that has already come up. Your insurer may ask for it later.

City pipe or your pipe? The line that decides who you call

Where the city's responsibility ends in Jacksonville Beach

Jacksonville Beach runs its own water and sewer through the Water Division of Public Works. That split matters at 11 at night. A backup in the street, or sewage rising in several homes on your block, is a city main problem, and the city lists an emergency line for water and sewer at 904-247-6278. The regular office, at City Hall on North Third Street, is 904-247-6241 on weekdays.

Everything inside your property line is yours to fix. That includes the lateral that runs from your house to the city connection. If the street looks fine and only your house is backing up, a plumber with a cable machine is the right call, and a quick call to the city line can confirm the main is not involved. Ask us if you are unsure and we will help you sort it out.

Snaking and cabling: how a drain actually gets cleared

Drain cleaning is not one tool. The cable has to match the pipe, and using the wrong one is how clogs come back in a week. A plumber reads the symptoms first, then chooses the machine.

Hand auger

A short hand-cranked coil for a trap or a few feet of tub or sink line. It clears hair and soap plugs without much risk to old pipe.

Drum machine with a small cable

A motorized drum feeds a thin cable through kitchen and bathroom branch lines. The cutting head spins through grease and sludge so the line flows full again.

Sectional cable machine

Heavy, stiff cable sections for the main drain and cleanout. It pushes through roots and packed debris where a small cable would only bend and stall.

Drain camera

A small camera on a flexible rod shows what is in the pipe when cabling does not explain the problem. It tells us whether you need a clearing or a repair.

A cable opens a hole through a clog, but it does not scrub the pipe wall. When grease or scale keeps rebuilding the plug, hydro jetting is the better tool because water pressure scours the full diameter of the line.

Kitchen sink clogs: the grease and starch problem

Kitchen lines clog differently from bathroom lines. Cooking fat runs down warm and liquid, then cools on the pipe wall and catches rice, pasta, coffee grounds and eggshell. Over months the bore shrinks to a thin channel, and one more handful of food scraps shuts it. That is why the sink often drains fine for weeks, then quits all at once.

If your disposal runs but the sink still stands full, the blockage is past the disposal. A plumber will usually pull the trap to check for a plug there, then cable the wall line. A disposal that hums and will not turn is a separate fix, and our team on garbage disposal repair covers that.

Slow drains, gurgling and a washer that backs up

Gurgling is air being pulled through a water seal because the pipe behind it is restricted. It is an early warning, not a nuisance. A drain that is slow at the bathroom sink but fine everywhere else usually has hair and toothpaste scum on the pop-up or in the trap, and a plumber can clear it quickly.

A washer that backs up into the laundry standpipe is a different matter. Washers move a lot of water fast, so a line that handles a sink can still overflow under a full discharge. If the standpipe overflows even when the line looks clear between loads, a camera inspection will show whether the main has a belly or a root intrusion.

Shower and tub drains, and drain lines that leak

Hair wraps on the crossbar or stopper, and soap binds it into a mat. Pulling the strainer and removing it often fixes a slow tub. If water rises around your ankles after that, the clog is deeper in the line and needs a cable.

A drain line that leaks is a repair, not a cleaning. Signs include a damp ceiling below a bathroom, a musty smell with no visible source, or water that disappears under a slab-edge wall. When a cable run turns up a cracked or separated section, we will show you the camera footage and quote the repair separately. For deeper lines, sewer line cleaning and camera inspection explain the next steps.
