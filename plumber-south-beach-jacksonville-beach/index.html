What the city says about South Beach

The City of Jacksonville Beach lists a South Beach (Southend) Redevelopment District of approximately 356 acres, near J. Turner Butler Boulevard and west of Third Street, which is State Road A1A. That is a planning district, so it covers many kinds of land. We could not find a city source that says how many homes are in it or when they were built, so this page does not make claims about the age of local plumbing.

South Beach Park is in this area, and the city's redevelopment report for 2018-19 mentions Fire Station 2 there. The same report is the main public record of drainage work in the south end.

The Southend stormwater system

The 2018-19 redevelopment report describes a project to enclose the open-ditch part of the Southend Stormwater System between J. Turner Butler Boulevard and Marsh Landing Parkway. It also describes funded drainage design for the Ocean Terrace area. The city runs all of this, and it is separate from the pipes on your property.

For a homeowner the useful point is where public drainage ends. Your sewer lateral runs from the house to the city main. If a drain backs up, a camera inspection tells you whether the cause is on your side. If the line is partly blocked by roots or grease, sewer line cleaning is usually the first step.

Not sure whose problem it is?

If neighbors on your street have the same backup, call City Hall at 904-247-6241 as well as a plumber. If only your home is affected, the cause is more likely inside your property line.

Homes, condos and water heaters

We do not have a verified count of homes or a housing mix for the south end, so we treat each address on its own. If you live in a condominium or townhome community, plumbing is usually split between the unit and the association, so ask your property manager what you are responsible for before you book a visit.

Common calls from the south end

  • A water bill that climbs with no change in use, see leak detection
  • A wet spot in the yard or low pressure, see water line repair
  • A bathroom or kitchen refit, see plumbing installation
