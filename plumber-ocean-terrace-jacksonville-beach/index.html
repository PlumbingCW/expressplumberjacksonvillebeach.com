What we know about Ocean Terrace

Real estate data lists Ocean Terrace at roughly 580 homes and a handful of vacant lots, about 88 percent of them single-family. The streets with the most homes are Palm Way, Grande Boulevard and America Avenue. Seaside Drive East also appears in listings. The same sources disagree on when the homes were built, with some saying the early 1980s and others showing many different years, so we do not assign the area a typical age.

We also could not confirm a formal boundary or a homeowners association from public sources. If your street is near the edge, give us the address and we will go by that.

Drainage: a named pond in city records

Ocean Terrace is named in city drainage records. A city stormwater update presented to council mentions Ocean Terrace Pond, and the city's redevelopment agency report for 2018-19 lists funded drainage design for Ocean Terrace. That is public infrastructure, and the city maintains it.

What it means for a homeowner is practical. When the ground is saturated after heavy rain, a lateral with a crack or a root intrusion can take on groundwater, and the symptoms look like a city drainage problem. A camera inspection shows which one you have. If the lateral is damaged, sewer line repair is the fix to ask about.

What homeowners here usually ask about

  • A tank water heater that is leaking or no longer heating, which is the usual reason for water heater replacement
  • A toilet that runs, clogs or rocks, handled under toilet repair
  • A kitchen drain that is slow or a disposal that has jammed, see garbage disposal repair
  • Hard-water spots or scale in fixtures, where water softener and filtration is the option to compare

City sewer and the septic phase-out rebate

The city provides water and sewer service for the Jacksonville Beach area. Where a home is still on septic, the city's phase-out program offers a rebate that covers actual costs up to $8,000, made up of a capital improvement fee, a tap-in fee and contractor costs. Whether your address qualifies is a question for City Hall. We cannot confirm any particular home's status.
