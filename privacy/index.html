What we collect

When you call, we may collect your phone number, your name, your service address and a description of the problem so a plumber can help you. Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

When you visit this website, standard server logs may record your IP address, browser type and the pages you open. The site has no forms and does not ask you to type personal details into a page.

How we use it

We use call details to schedule and complete your service, to improve our dispatch process and to answer billing questions. We do not sell your personal information.

Who sees it

Your details are shared with the plumber assigned to your job and with service providers who help us run the phone line. We may disclose information when the law requires it.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, or ask us to delete it where the law allows, by emailing contact@expressplumberjacksonvillebeach.com or calling (904) 561-8133.
